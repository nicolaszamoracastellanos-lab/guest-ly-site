№ 01What you get

Three promises.

  • Web chat and WhatsApp. An elegant chat page on a link, plus a dedicated WhatsApp number guests text like any contact. Nothing to install, no accounts to create: they tap a link or scan a QR code and ask away.
  • Wedding website with per-person RSVPs. A bilingual site with your story, itinerary, dress code and registry. Each guest confirms each person for each event, from the site or straight in the chat.
  • Travel answers built in. Visas, currency, weather, transport and local tips for guests flying in from abroad.
  • QR guest passes. Every guest gets a personal QR pass for a smooth check-in at the door.
  • Their language. Fully bilingual in English and Spanish, with best-effort answers in other languages. SMS and Telegram are coming soon.
  • Wedding Brain. Everything your concierge knows, editable by you. Change a hotel tip, preview the answer, publish instantly.
  • Live dashboard. Every question guests ask, the topics they care about, RSVP momentum, and the gaps your Brain has not covered yet.
  • Guest list and RSVP board. Import from Excel, CSV or Zola in one click. Every response with per-event detail, automatic reminders to non-responders, and a Monday digest in your inbox.
  • Broadcasts. Message all guests, only non-responders, or one group, on WhatsApp, in each guest’s own language.
  • Website studio. Edit your wedding site live: sections, themes, photos, custom RSVP questions. Publish when it is perfect.
  • AI Coordinator. The optional add-on: tell it what changed, in plain English or Spanish, and it updates the list, the RSVPs and the reminders. Every change asks for your approval first.
  • Trained only on your wedding. The concierge answers from your wedding’s information alone. When it is not sure, it tells the guest it is checking with you and flags the question on your dashboard.
  • Full transcripts. You can read every conversation your guests have with the concierge, any time.
  • Zero setup for you. We handle the WhatsApp business approval, the hosting, the servers and the monitoring. You never touch code or create a business account.
  • Version history. Every change to your Wedding Brain is versioned, with one-click rollback.
  • Live in about a week. From intake form to live concierge in about seven days. Signature and Grande include priority support. Guest limits count invited people, not messages.
