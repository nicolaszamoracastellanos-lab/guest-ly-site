## 1. Campaign Overview

- **Campaign name:** "The Wedding Bot That Ran My Own Wedding"
- **One-line summary:** Use the live Alex & Nico production bot as proof, build credibility on Instagram and LinkedIn, and convert attention into 3 founding clients and a planner pipeline.
- **Primary objective:** 3 founding bot clients committed within 60 days of first post.
- **Secondary objectives:** 5 planner discovery conversations; a credible active profile; a reusable content library.

**Strategic constraint (unchanged):** social does not close founding clients, DMs do. Social exists so outreach lands. Every post must make a couple or planner think "this is real and it works."

---

## 2. Target Audience

Unchanged from v1.

**Primary: The Destination Couple.** Engaged, 26 to 38, 30%+ of guests traveling internationally. LatAm destination weddings as beachhead plus US couples with bilingual guest lists. Pain: the same 40 questions over WhatsApp for months. They already have Zola; Guest-ly is the layer on top. Found on Instagram, Pinterest, wedding subreddits, planner referrals.

**Secondary: The Wedding Planner.** Independent and boutique destination planners. Pain: they ARE the concierge, at 11pm. Pitch: makes them look high-tech and cuts message volume. Found on Instagram and LinkedIn.

**Tertiary: Build-in-Public (LinkedIn).** GTM and AI builders. They amplify and refer, they do not buy.

## 8. Success Metrics

Targets unchanged from v1:

| Metric | 30-day | 60-day |
|---|---|---|
| Founding client conversations (primary) | 5 started | 3 committed |
| Planner conversations | 2 | 5 |
| IG followers | 150 | 400 |
| "GUEST" demo DMs | 10 | 30 |
| Saves + shares per carousel | 15 avg | 30 avg |
| LinkedIn avg impressions | 2,000 | 5,000 |
| Profile-to-site clicks | 40 | 120 |

**The day-60 failure case, defined (new in v2).** If fewer than 3 founding clients are committed by day 60:

1. **Diagnose before changing anything**, using the funnel numbers: (a) reach problem (under 20 total demo DMs): content is not landing, fix distribution. (b) offer problem (20+ DMs, under 5 serious conversations): the free-bot offer or its framing is off, fix the pitch. (c) conversion problem (5+ serious conversations, no commits): the demo or trust is failing, fix the close.
2. **Actions by diagnosis:** (a) Reach: content cadence stays but shifts 100% to Pillar 1 and 2; add 30 min/week engaging in planner Facebook groups and wedding subreddits; do NOT increase content hours. (b) Offer: pivot the free offer from couples to PLANNERS (one free bot for a planner's next destination wedding reaches many couples at once). (c) Conversion: add a live 15-minute walkthrough call to the DM loop instead of async demo only.
3. **What does not happen:** content spend does not increase, paid ads do not start, and the build roadmap (intake generator) does not pause. At zero clients, content volume is never the bottleneck; the offer and the outreach are.
4. **Hard review at day 75:** if still zero commits after the day-60 adjustment, cadence drops to maintenance (2 posts/week) and the freed hours move entirely to direct planner outreach (target: 50 personalized DMs over 3 weeks).
